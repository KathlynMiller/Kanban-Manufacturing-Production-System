Statuses that can apply at any stage — confirmed at kickoff

Waiting for Raw Material Rework Hold Cancelled Rework routes an order back depending on the issue found at Deflash — not a simple forward skip. Operators trigger every stage move manually; there is no automatic system event.

Timing rules — confirmed at kickoff

Start / Stop timestampsEvery phase gets a standardized start and stop timestamp, called out specifically for Deflash.
Cycle-time target4–5 days for a full production run. Longer than that counts as overdue — a separate signal from missing the due date.
Time in limboTracks how long an order sits idle *between* phases, not just time spent actively in one.
Bottleneck watchBack Log builds up ahead of Deflash and Production — the two queues to watch first.

What counts as WIP

WIP Orders measure Back Log Production Deflash Queue Deflash To Do Final Inspection Queue Final Inspection Packaging Awaiting Ship Shipped Back Log (not started) and Shipped (finished) are excluded — the dashboard's WIP Orders DAX measure counts everything actively moving between them.
